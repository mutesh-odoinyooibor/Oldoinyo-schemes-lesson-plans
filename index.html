Curriculum library

Every strand and sub-strand from the KICD Grade 7, 8 and 9 curriculum designs (April 2024), with the number of lessons each design suggests. Pick your area; the grade comes from step 1.

The designs cover the whole year and do not say which sub-strands go in which term. “This term’s share” takes an even third of the lessons in the design’s order, so move or remove rows to match your own plan. Names and lesson numbers were read from the KICD files, so check them against your copy.

Lesson notes

Each lesson gets its own page of notes for learners to copy into their exercise books. Lines starting Heading: or Subheading: print underlined, and where a drawing helps, a line starting Diagram: leaves a framed box to draw it in. Choose a period, generate, edit the notes, then download.
